      ## **Career Services**


      Launch your career! [Career Services](http://careerservices.wvu.edu/) will
      help with internships and your job search. Starting your first semester,
      we are ready to help with:


      * creating a résumé finding internships

      * hosting career fairs with hundreds of potential employers

      * Handshake, our digital career resource, has 7,000+ jobs/internships
      posted annually


      ## **Purpose Center**


      We have the first [Purpose Center](https://purpose.wvu.edu/) on any
      college campus. We’ll help you learn your strengths and how to use them to
      reach your full potential. CliftonStrengths® is a fun (no stress) online
      tool you can use, for free, along with individual coaching sessions.


      ## **Tutoring**


      College is different from high school. Even if you’ve taken college-level
      classes, there will be an adjustment. That’s why we offer free
      [tutoring](https://tutoring.wvu.edu/) and [student success
      coaching](https://studentsuccess.wvu.edu/reach-for-success/success-coaching).


      ## **Student Success Programs**


      Your goal is graduation. And we want you to get there. Our [Student
      Success programs](http://studentsuccess.wvu.edu/) will help you stay on
      goal with free services like mentoring, workshops and programs especially
      for first-generation students and students of color.


      ## **Advising**


      Your academic adviser will help you chart a plan of study. Depending on
      your major, your adviser will be a faculty member in your department or a
      professional adviser familiar with your major and possible career paths.
      If you haven’t decided on a major, you will work with an
      [adviser](https://advisingcenter.wvu.edu/) to narrow your academic and
      career interests. You can also use
      [DegreeWorks](http://registrar.wvu.edu/dw) to get real-time advice and
      track progress toward your degree.
